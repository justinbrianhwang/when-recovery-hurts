I found three concrete issues, ranked by severity. This is a source review only; I could not run the simulator locally.

1. **High — `run` accepts a budget gate from another plan.** [30_recovery_v1_diag.py:261](scripts/30_recovery_v1_diag.py:261) checks `fits_cap` but not the budget gate’s plan hash. A stale passing gate can authorize the single-use diagnostic under a different plan. **Fix:** require `budget_gate.json["plan_sha256"] == sha256(a.plan)` before writing the ledger.

2. **Medium — an unconfirmed loss of grasp is labelled as still holding.** [recovery_v1.py:125](src/hci/recovery_v1.py:125) treats one or two final steps with `is_cubeA_grasped == False` as a hold, then may emit `NOT_RELEASED_AT_PLACE` or `HOLDING_NOT_PLACED`. The trace does not establish either claim. **Fix:** distinguish a grasp true on the final row from a short, unresolved final gap; label the latter `UNKNOWN` with its gap length.

3. **Medium — release evidence is timestamped at the last grasped step.** [recovery_v1.py:137](src/hci/recovery_v1.py:137)–[140](src/hci/recovery_v1.py:140) records `release_step`, position, height and contact force from `b`, although the first observed loss is `b + 1`. A cube moving across a tolerance boundary on that step can get the wrong release mechanism label. **Fix:** record both last-grasped and first-not-grasped measurements, and base the release label on an explicitly chosen observation step.
